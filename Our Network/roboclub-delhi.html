About RoboClub

RoboClub is a dynamic technology and robotics community based in Delhi, bringing together young innovators, engineers, and makers who are passionate about building the future. Through workshops, competitions, and hands-on projects, RoboClub nurtures the next generation of technologists.

As a Battery Bin partner, RoboClub brings a uniquely fitting perspective to battery recycling — its members understand better than most exactly what's inside a battery and why responsible disposal matters. The collection point at RoboClub serves both the community and the cause.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Active robotics and technology community in Delhi
  • Members with deep hands-on knowledge of electronics and batteries
  • Culture of innovation naturally aligned with sustainable practices
  • Battery Bin integrated into the club's workspace and events
  • A partnership that bridges technology and environmental responsibility

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Who better to champion battery recycling than people who build robots? RoboClub members work with batteries, motors, and circuits daily — and they know firsthand the chemical complexity packed into every cell. That knowledge makes their commitment to proper disposal all the more genuine.

Through member-led awareness sessions and by hosting a Battery Bin drop point, RoboClub is helping spread a critical message: the same technology that powers innovation must be handled responsibly at the end of its life.

🗣️ What People Say

"We use batteries in almost every project. Knowing there's a safe place to dispose of them here makes our workspace more responsible."
"Technology and sustainability aren't opposites — they go together. RoboClub's Battery Bin partnership is proof of that."

🔍 Did You Know?

Electronic components like batteries contain lithium, cadmium, and lead — materials that RoboClub members work with regularly. Their technical understanding makes them among Battery Bin's most informed and passionate advocates.
