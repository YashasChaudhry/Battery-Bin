About the Institution

Vivekananda Institute of Professional Studies (VIPS), located in Pitampura, Delhi, is one of the capital's leading professional education institutions. Affiliated with GGSIPU, VIPS offers programmes across law, management, computer applications, and commerce — nurturing thousands of students each year into career-ready professionals.

As a Battery Bin partner, VIPS hosts a dedicated battery collection point on campus, channelling its vibrant student community toward meaningful, everyday environmental action.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Premier GGSIPU-affiliated institution in North Delhi
  • Thousands of students across law, management, and technology programmes
  • Strong culture of student-led social and civic initiatives
  • Battery Bin placed in a high-footfall area of the campus
  • One of Battery Bin's first higher-education partners in Delhi

♻️ Sustainability in Action

VIPS students are tomorrow's lawyers, managers, and technologists — and at VIPS, they're also learning to be responsible citizens. The Battery Bin partnership gives the student community a tangible, daily way to act on their environmental values.

From awareness campaigns to collection drives, VIPS integrates battery recycling into its broader culture of civic engagement — proving that professional education and environmental responsibility go hand in hand.

🗣️ What People Say

"I study law and I know how damaging improper battery disposal can be. Having the Battery Bin right here makes it so easy to do the right thing."
"Such a small habit — dropping used batteries in the bin — but knowing it protects our groundwater makes it feel really meaningful."

🔍 Did You Know?

VIPS's large campus population means it has the potential to divert hundreds of batteries from Delhi's landfills every single year — making it one of Battery Bin's most impactful urban partners.
