About the College

Vivekananda Institute of Professional Studies (VIPS), located in Pitampura on the Outer Ring Road in North Delhi, is one of the capital's most respected private institutions. Established in 1998 under the Strength India Educational Society, it is affiliated with Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU) and holds a prestigious NAAC A++ accreditation — the highest grade awarded by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council.

As a proud Battery Bin partner, VIPS hosts a battery collection point on campus, encouraging thousands of students, faculty, and visitors to safely dispose of used batteries and take a concrete step toward environmental responsibility.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • NAAC A++ accredited — among the highest-ranked private colleges in Delhi
  • Eco-friendly, Wi-Fi enabled, five-acre green campus in Pitampura
  • Strong culture of innovation with an active IPR Cell and research programs
  • Direct Metro connectivity via Samaypur Badli Mor for easy campus access
  • Battery Bin placed in a prominent, high-footfall area on campus

♻️ Sustainability in Action

VIPS has long championed a green, eco-conscious campus ethos. Its five-acre grounds are designed with sustainability in mind — and the Battery Bin partnership takes that commitment further. With thousands of students passing through the campus daily across programs in Engineering, Law, Management, Media, and more, the Battery Bin at VIPS has the potential to collect a significant volume of hazardous batteries that would otherwise end up in landfills.

Student-led awareness campaigns, eco clubs, and peer-to-peer outreach make recycling a normal part of campus life. Every battery deposited is a small act with a large collective impact.

🗣️ What Students Say

♻️ Why This Matters

Colleges like VIPS shape the minds and habits of tomorrow's professionals. By bringing the Battery Bin onto campus, VIPS is reinforcing a simple truth: sustainability isn't just a subject on a syllabus — it is a daily practice. Each battery collected here prevents toxic chemicals like lead, mercury, and cadmium from polluting our soil and water, making the campus partnership one of the most impactful in the Battery Bin network.
