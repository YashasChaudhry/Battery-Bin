I.T.S Dental College, Hospital & Research Centre

Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

About the Institution

I.T.S Dental College, Hospital & Research Centre, located in Greater Noida's Knowledge Park, is a premier dental institution under the I.T.S – The Education Group. Offering BDS and MDS programmes, the college combines clinical excellence with cutting-edge research to produce dental professionals of the highest calibre.

As part of the I.T.S group's Battery Bin partnership, the Dental College extends Battery Bin's reach into Greater Noida — a rapidly growing educational hub. Its location in Knowledge Park places it at the centre of one of the NCR's densest concentrations of students and academic professionals.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Premier dental institution in Greater Noida's Knowledge Park
  • Part of the NAAC A+ accredited I.T.S – The Education Group
  • Clinicians and researchers who understand the chemistry of hazardous materials
  • Large student and patient-visitor footfall creates wide collection impact
  • Battery Bin placed in a prominent area accessible to students and visitors

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Healthcare and dental professionals work with hazardous materials daily — and they understand, perhaps better than most, what happens when such materials are improperly disposed of. I.T.S Dental College's Battery Bin partnership is a natural extension of the care and precision that defines medical education.

Students, faculty, patients, and visitors all pass through I.T.S Dental College's campus — creating a diverse community of people who can carry the battery recycling habit back to their homes, clinics, and neighbourhoods.

🗣️ What People Say

"In dentistry we learn exactly how toxic heavy metals can be in the body. Batteries contain those same metals — they should never go in regular waste."
"The Battery Bin at our campus is a small reminder that environmental care is part of patient care."

🔍 Did You Know?

I.T.S Dental College is the third institution in the I.T.S group to join the Battery Bin network — making I.T.S one of the single largest contributors to Battery Bin's collection capacity across the NCR.
