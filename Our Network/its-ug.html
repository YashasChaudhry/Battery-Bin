    About the Institution

    I.T.S UG Institute, located in Mohan Nagar, Ghaziabad, is an undergraduate institution under the prestigious I.T.S – The Education Group. Offering undergraduate programmes across commerce, arts, and science, the institute serves a large and diverse student body in the heart of the NCR.

    As part of the broader I.T.S Battery Bin partnership, the UG Institute hosts a battery collection point that brings environmental responsibility to its undergraduate community — students who are at a formative stage in developing their civic values and habits.

    🌱 Why It Stands Out

    • Part of the NAAC A+ accredited I.T.S – The Education Group
    • Large undergraduate student body across multiple disciplines
    • Vibrant campus culture with strong extracurricular engagement
    • Battery Bin placed centrally within the institute campus
    • Shares the I.T.S group's commitment to quality and responsibility

    ♻️ Sustainability in Action

    Undergraduate students are at precisely the right stage of life to build lasting habits — and the I.T.S UG Institute's Battery Bin partnership ensures that responsible battery disposal is one of them. For many students, this may be their first conscious environmental action.

    The institute actively encourages students to participate in collection drives and spread awareness among family and friends — turning the campus Battery Bin into a ripple effect that reaches far beyond the college gates.

    🗣️ What People Say

    "I never thought twice about throwing a battery in the bin. Now I know the difference — and so do my friends at home."
    "It's great that our college takes this seriously. It makes us feel like our small actions actually count."

    🔍 Did You Know?

    I.T.S UG Institute is part of a group of three I.T.S campuses in the Battery Bin network — making the I.T.S group one of Battery Bin's largest single institutional partners anywhere in the NCR.
