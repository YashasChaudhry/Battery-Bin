About the College

Sushila Inter College for Girls, located in East Model Town, Nehru Nagar, Ghaziabad, is one of the region's most established institutions for girls' education. Founded in 1958 by Late Shri Jai Prakash in fond memory of his sister Smt. Sushila Devi, the college has grown from a handful of students to a thriving community of over 2,700 learners across Humanities, Science, and Commerce streams.

As a proud Battery Bin partner, the college hosts a dedicated battery collection point that empowers students and staff to responsibly dispose of used batteries — turning everyday campus life into an act of environmental stewardship.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Over six decades of dedicated girls' education in Ghaziabad
  • 2,700+ students across Science, Commerce, and Humanities streams (Classes VI–XII)
  • State-of-the-art Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer labs
  • 300-seat Mukand Hall for cultural programmes and events
  • Battery Bin placed in a visible, high-footfall area on campus

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Through its partnership with Battery Bin, Sushila Inter College is channelling the energy of thousands of young women toward a greener future. Students participate in battery collection drives, spread awareness among peers and families, and take part in real-world recycling action that extends well beyond the classroom.

With more than 98% of students clearing their examinations and 32% achieving first-division results, this college breeds high achievers — and high achievers lead change.
