About the Society

Ashiana Le Residency is a premium residential society within the LandCraft township in Ghaziabad. Known for its well-maintained infrastructure, thoughtful amenities, and a close-knit resident community, it reflects the aspirations of families who care as much about their environment as they do about their homes.

As a Battery Bin partner, Ashiana Le Residency brings safe battery disposal directly to the doorstep of its residents. The collection point within the society means that families no longer need to wonder where their old batteries go — they can drop them off safely, right where they live.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Premium residential society with environmentally conscious families
  • Battery Bin accessible to residents within their own community
  • High concentration of households means significant collection potential
  • Resident welfare committees actively support environmental initiatives
  • Brings battery recycling into daily domestic life, where it matters most

🗣️ What People Say

"We generate a surprising number of dead batteries at home. Having the bin right here in our society means we actually recycle them instead of throwing them away."
"My kids now remind me to save the old batteries for the Battery Bin. It's become a habit — and a great lesson for them too."

🔍 Did You Know?

The average Indian household discards over 10 batteries per year. In a society like Ashiana Le Residency with hundreds of families, that adds up to thousands of batteries annually — every one of which can be safely recycled instead of contaminating the local environment.
