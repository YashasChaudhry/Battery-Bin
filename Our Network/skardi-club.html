Skardi Club

LandCraft, Ghaziabad

About the Club

Skardi Club is the recreational and social hub of the Golflinks LandCraft township in Ghaziabad — a vibrant community space where residents gather for leisure, sport, and everyday connection. With a membership drawn from one of the area's most engaged residential communities, the club is a natural meeting point for families who care about the world around them.

As a Battery Bin partner, Skardi Club makes responsible battery disposal effortless for LandCraft residents. With collection points conveniently placed within the club premises, members can drop off used batteries during their regular visits — turning a routine trip into an environmental act.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Centrally located within a large, well-planned residential township
  • High daily footfall from residents using club facilities
  • Community-driven environment naturally aligned with sustainability values
  • Multiple Battery Bin points placed in prominent, accessible locations
  • Extends green living from the home into shared community spaces

🗣️ What People Say

"Having the Battery Bin right here at the club makes it so easy. I just carry my old batteries on my way to the gym and drop them off."
"It's a small thing, but it's the kind of initiative that makes you proud to be part of this community."

🔍 Did You Know?

A single AA battery can contaminate up to 1 square metre of soil for 50 years. In a township of hundreds of homes, proper disposal at a convenient community hub like Skardi Club can prevent tonnes of toxic material from entering the local environment.
