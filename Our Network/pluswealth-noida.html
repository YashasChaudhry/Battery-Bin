About Plus Wealth

Plus Wealth is a professional financial services firm based in Noida, committed to helping individuals and businesses build secure financial futures. With a team of experienced advisors and a client-first philosophy, Plus Wealth has established itself as a trusted name in the Noida business community.

As a Battery Bin partner, Plus Wealth demonstrates that environmental responsibility is as important as financial responsibility. The battery collection point at their Noida office encourages staff, clients, and visitors to make sustainable choices as part of everyday life.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Established financial services firm in the Noida business community
  • Client-facing office with consistent daily footfall
  • Commitment to corporate responsibility alongside financial advisory
  • Battery Bin accessible to staff, clients, and walk-in visitors
  • One of Battery Bin's first corporate sector partners in Noida

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Businesses have a responsibility that goes beyond their balance sheets — and Plus Wealth understands this well. By hosting a Battery Bin collection point, the firm signals to clients and staff alike that good stewardship applies to the environment just as much as it applies to investments.

Plus Wealth actively encourages its team to bring used batteries from home, and places information about battery recycling in client-facing areas — turning a financial services office into a small but meaningful hub for environmental awareness.

🗣️ What People Say

"We advise clients to think long-term with their money. The same logic applies to the environment — what we do today matters decades from now."
"It's such a simple thing to have a Battery Bin in the office. It says a lot about a firm's values."

🔍 Did You Know?

Corporate offices collectively consume enormous quantities of batteries — in remotes, devices, emergency lighting, and more. Plus Wealth's Battery Bin ensures that none of those batteries end up in landfill.
