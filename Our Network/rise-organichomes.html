About Rise Organic Homes

Rise Organic Homes is a premium residential apartment society developed by the Rise Group, located along the Delhi–Meerut Expressway (NH-24) near Lal Kuan, Ghaziabad. Designed with a focus on modern living and community well-being, it is home to hundreds of families who share a commitment to quality, sustainability, and a cleaner environment.

As a Battery Bin partner, Rise Organic Homes brings responsible battery recycling directly into the daily lives of its residents. With a collection point placed within the society premises, families can now dispose of used batteries safely and conveniently — without leaving their neighbourhood.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Large residential community with high daily footfall from families and individuals
  • Conveniently located on the Delhi–Meerut Expressway, serving a well-connected community
  • Battery Bin placed in an accessible common area within the society
  • Residents are directly invested in keeping their living environment clean and safe
  • Partnership brings environmental responsibility home — literally

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Residential societies are one of the most powerful places to drive environmental change — because the people who live there are directly affected by the quality of their surroundings. Rise Organic Homes residents don't just care about clean common areas; they care about the soil, water, and air their families share every day.

Battery Bin's presence at Rise Organic Homes means that used AA batteries, TV remotes, toys, torches, and other household battery sources no longer need to end up in the dustbin. Residents can collect them at home and drop them off at the Battery Bin — a habit that protects the environment one household at a time.

🗣️ What Residents Say

"We always wanted a responsible way to dispose of the batteries our kids go through. Now it's as easy as dropping them off downstairs."
"A small bin in our society — but the impact goes far beyond these walls."

🔍 Did You Know?

A single AA battery contains mercury, cadmium, and lead — enough to contaminate up to 600,000 litres of water if disposed of in a landfill. Residential societies like Rise Organic Homes are at the frontline of preventing this, one battery drop-off at a time.
