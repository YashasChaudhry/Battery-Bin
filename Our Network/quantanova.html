About Quantanova

Quantanova Technologies is an innovative technology company based in Gurgaon, working at the intersection of data, software, and intelligent systems. With a team of sharp engineers and problem-solvers, Quantanova builds solutions that push boundaries — and partners with Battery Bin to push boundaries on sustainability too.

As a Battery Bin partner, Quantanova Technologies brings the tech sector into the environmental conversation. The battery collection point at their Gurgaon office ensures that the batteries powering the devices behind their innovation are disposed of with the same intelligence they bring to everything else.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Innovative tech company at the forefront of Gurgaon's startup ecosystem
  • Team of engineers who understand the lifecycle of electronic components
  • Gurgaon office with consistent team and visitor footfall
  • Battery Bin integrated into the workspace as part of responsible office culture
  • One of Battery Bin's first technology sector partners

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Technology companies consume batteries at scale — in laptops, peripherals, testing equipment, and the dozens of devices that keep a modern tech office running. Quantanova's Battery Bin partnership ensures that none of those batteries end up polluting the environment.

Beyond simple collection, Quantanova's team actively discusses the importance of e-waste responsibility — bringing the same analytical rigour to environmental questions that they bring to technical ones. Sustainability, in their view, is an engineering problem worth solving.

🗣️ What People Say

"We build systems that are supposed to make life better. Disposing of batteries properly is the least we can do to make sure we're not making things worse."
"Quantanova cares about impact — not just product impact, but environmental impact. The Battery Bin is a reflection of that."

🔍 Did You Know?

The technology sector is one of the fastest-growing contributors to e-waste globally. By partnering with Battery Bin, Quantanova Technologies is part of a growing movement of tech firms choosing to lead on environmental responsibility rather than wait for regulation.
