About the Club

Golf Links Club is a premier recreational and social club in Ghaziabad, offering members a world-class sporting and leisure experience. With a distinguished membership that values quality, community, and the outdoors, Golf Links Club is a natural partner for an initiative dedicated to protecting the environment.

As a Battery Bin partner, Golf Links Club brings sustainability to one of Ghaziabad's most influential social communities. The battery collection point at the club ensures that members — who already care deeply about maintaining their green surroundings — can extend that care to responsible battery disposal.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Premier recreational club with a distinguished, engaged membership
  • Strong appreciation for environmental preservation among members
  • High-footfall social space ideal for community-wide awareness
  • Battery Bin placed in a prominent, accessible location within the club
  • A partnership that extends green values from the fairway to daily life

♻️ Sustainability in Action

A golf club is, by its very nature, a community that values green spaces and the natural environment. Golf Links Club's partnership with Battery Bin is a natural extension of those values — ensuring that the chemicals in discarded batteries never find their way into the soil and water that members and their families enjoy.

Through member communications and the club's own social channels, Golf Links Club actively encourages its community to bring used batteries to the collection point — creating a ripple effect that reaches households across Ghaziabad's most engaged social circles.

🗣️ What People Say

"We invest so much in maintaining our greens. The Battery Bin is a reminder that protecting the environment starts with the small things too."
"I brought a bag of old batteries from home after seeing the bin here. It takes two minutes and makes a real difference."

🔍 Did You Know?

Social clubs are powerful vectors for behaviour change — when respected community members adopt a habit, others follow. Golf Links Club's Battery Bin partnership is as much about cultural influence as it is about collection numbers.
