About the School

Step by Step School, Noida, is one of the leading educational institutions in the region, known for its focus on holistic learning, academic excellence, and environmental responsibility. The school actively participates in sustainability initiatives, encouraging students to take part in real-world problem solving.

As part of the Battery Bin network, Step by Step School is committed to safe battery disposal, making recycling accessible for students and staff.

🌱 Green Leadership

The school fosters a strong sense of environmental stewardship among its students. By hosting a Battery Bin on campus, Step by Step is not only teaching sustainability but also enabling action that protects the environment.

⚡ URJA Science Fest – Step by Step School, Noida

On Tuesday, 12th August, Battery Bin participated in the URJA Science Fest in collaboration with the school's Environment Club. We set up our own stall to raise awareness about battery recycling and safe disposal.

🎯 Activities & Highlights
  • Interactive game on common battery misconceptions — participants scoring above a threshold won chocolates.
  • Distributed awareness posters and engaged students in discussions about Battery Bin and battery safety.
  • Demonstrated a small machine that could measure remaining battery charge (full, half-full, empty).
  • Launched on-campus battery collection — 22 batteries collected on the event day.
  • Key interaction: The Head of the Science Department, Dr. Pratibha Menon, visited our stall.
  • Major contributions by Yashas – led the event, designed the game, organized and set up the stall, and managed all activities.
