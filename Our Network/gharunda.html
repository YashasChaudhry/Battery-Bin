About Gharunda

Gharunda is a community-rooted NGO based in Ghaziabad, working to create safe, nurturing spaces for underprivileged children and families. The name itself — meaning 'home' in Hindi — reflects the organisation's core belief: that every child deserves a warm, supportive environment in which to grow.

As a Battery Bin partner, Gharunda brings environmental education to some of Ghaziabad's most underserved communities — proving that the message of sustainability is not a privilege, but a right that belongs to everyone.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Community-driven NGO with deep roots in Ghaziabad's underserved neighbourhoods
  • Focus on child welfare, education, and community upliftment
  • Brings environmental awareness to communities that rarely receive it
  • Battery Bin serves as both a collection point and a teaching tool
  • A partnership grounded in the belief that sustainability is for everyone

♻️ Sustainability in Action

At Gharunda, the Battery Bin isn't just a recycling point — it's a conversation starter. For families who have never had access to formal environmental education, the simple act of dropping a used battery into the bin opens up a whole new way of thinking about how everyday objects affect the world around us.

Gharunda's volunteers and community workers actively use the Battery Bin as part of their broader awareness sessions — teaching children and parents alike why batteries must never be thrown in regular waste, and what happens when they are.

🗣️ What People Say

"Nobody ever told us that batteries could poison the soil. Now we know, and we make sure every battery goes in the right place."
"Gharunda has taught us so much — and the Battery Bin is one more way they're helping us take care of our community."

🔍 Did You Know?

NGOs like Gharunda reach communities that formal institutions often miss. Through this partnership, Battery Bin is ensuring that responsible disposal becomes a habit not just in schools and colleges, but in homes and neighbourhoods across Ghaziabad.
