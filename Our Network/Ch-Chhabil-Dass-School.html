About the School

Ch. Chhabil Dass Public School (CCDPS), located in Patel Nagar, Ghaziabad, is one of the city's most established and respected CBSE-affiliated institutions. Founded in 1992 by the Chaudhary family under the aegis of the Smt. Sushila Devi Chhabil Dass Charitable Trust, the school has spent over three decades providing quality, affordable education to students from Nursery through Class XII.

As a proud Battery Bin partner, the school hosts dedicated battery collection points across its campus and has opened its classrooms to Battery Bin awareness sessions — equipping students with the knowledge to make responsible choices about battery disposal.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • CBSE-affiliated co-educational school, Nursery to Class XII
  • Four high-tech computer labs and a multimedia room, in association with NIIT
  • Digital Library with 3,500+ hours of multimedia content across all subjects
  • School bus transport available for all students
  • Security cameras installed across the campus
  • Multiple Battery Bin collection points placed across campus blocks

📚 Classroom Awareness Sessions

Battery Bin visited CCDPS to conduct in-class awareness sessions, speaking directly to students about the dangers of improper battery disposal and how they can make a difference. From understanding battery chemistry to learning how recycling protects our soil and water — students engaged with real questions and left knowing they could act.

📋 Awareness Poster

A Battery Bin awareness poster has been put up on the school notice board — a constant visual reminder for students, staff, and visitors about the importance of responsible battery disposal.

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Through its partnership with Battery Bin, CCDPS is channelling the curiosity and energy of its students toward a greener future. With collection bins placed across multiple campus blocks and awareness woven into classroom sessions, recycling is now part of everyday life at the school.

Every recycled battery is a step toward a cleaner, healthier Ghaziabad.

🔍 Did You Know?

CCDPS was set up in 1992 with a mission to make quality education accessible to all — including under-privileged students at subsidised costs. Today, that same spirit of giving back extends to Battery Bin's mission of protecting the environment for future generations.
