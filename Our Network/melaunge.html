About Melaunge

Melaunge By Vasavi is a beloved artisan bakery and café based in Gurgaon, known for its handcrafted bakes, warm atmosphere, and community spirit. Founded with a passion for quality and connection, Melaunge has become a cherished neighbourhood destination for food lovers across Gurgaon.

As a Battery Bin partner, Melaunge By Vasavi proves that sustainability belongs in every corner of community life — not just schools and offices. The battery collection point at the bakery gives customers an effortless way to do something good for the environment while enjoying something good for the soul.

🌱 Why It Stands Out

  • Beloved artisan bakery with a loyal, community-minded customer base
  • Warm, welcoming space that naturally fosters civic conversations
  • High daily footfall creates excellent collection opportunity
  • Battery Bin integrated thoughtfully into the customer experience
  • Battery Bin's first bakery partner — and one of its most delightful

♻️ Sustainability in Action

Cafés and bakeries are the heartbeat of a neighbourhood — places where people linger, connect, and share. Melaunge By Vasavi's Battery Bin partnership turns those everyday moments into opportunities for environmental action, making sustainability feel as natural and warm as the bread fresh from the oven.

Customers are encouraged to bring used batteries from home when they visit — and many do. The Battery Bin at Melaunge has become a small but beloved part of the café experience, reflecting the owner's belief that a good business takes care of both its community and its planet.

🗣️ What People Say

"I come here for the sourdough every week. Now I bring my old batteries too — it's become part of my Saturday routine."
"Vasavi's passion for her craft extends to caring for the environment. That's the kind of business I want to support."

🔍 Did You Know?

Neighbourhood businesses like Melaunge reach customers that institutions never can — people of all ages, backgrounds, and walks of life, united by a love of good food and community. That makes this one of Battery Bin's most uniquely impactful partnerships.
