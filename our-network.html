🌊 Initiative

NautiClean

Delhi NCR  ·  Active Collaboration

An autonomous marine trash collector using GPS navigation, Arduino + Raspberry Pi controls, and Particle Swarm Optimisation for path efficiency. Batteries recovered from polluted water bodies are channelled directly into Battery Bin's recycling network.

🏛️ Municipal Initiative Govt. Waste Pickup Truck Ghaziabad  ·  Active Collaboration

The municipal waste collection truck serving Ghaziabad now carries a Battery Bin awareness poster — reaching hundreds of households on every route. Batteries segregated during wet/dry/hazardous sorting are handed to Battery Bin for formal recycling instead of being sold to informal recyclers.
